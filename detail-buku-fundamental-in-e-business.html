Yulius Hari, Arief Budiman & Minny Elisa Yanggah

About The Book

Fundamental in E-Business: Theoretical and Practical Approach provides an essential framework for understanding and implementing e-business strategies. The book blends theoretical concepts with actionable practical methods for students, entrepreneurs, and business leaders in the digital economy.

  • In-Depth Theoretical Principles: Clear explanations of e-business models, market dynamics, and electronic commerce theory.
  • Actionable Practical Strategies: Step-by-step guides, checklists, and implementation templates for e-business operations.
  • Integrated Case Studies: Real-world examples of successful e-business deployment across various industries.
